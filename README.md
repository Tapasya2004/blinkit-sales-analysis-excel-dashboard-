# Blinkit Sales Analysis & Excel Dashboard

## Summary

An Excel-based sales analysis dashboard developed to evaluate Blinkit's sales performance, outlet characteristics, product performance, and customer ratings through interactive KPIs and visualizations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and helps identify sales patterns across item types, fat content, outlet size, outlet location, outlet type, and outlet establishment year.

## Business Objective

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution to identify key insights and opportunities for business optimization.

The dashboard focuses on four primary KPIs:

- **Total Sales** – Overall revenue generated from items sold.
- **Average Sales** – Average revenue per sale.
- **Number of Items** – Total number of items represented in the analysis.
- **Average Rating** – Average customer rating for the items sold.

---

## Business Problem

Blinkit needs a consolidated view of its sales performance across different product and outlet dimensions.

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

- Which item types contribute the most to total sales?
- How does fat content affect sales performance?
- How do sales vary across outlet sizes?
- Which outlet locations generate higher sales?
- How does outlet establishment year relate to sales?
- How do key KPIs vary across different outlet types?
- Which product and outlet segments represent stronger sales performance?

The dashboard brings these dimensions together into a single analytical view for easier comparison and decision-making.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content

**Objective:**  
Analyze the impact of fat content on total sales and compare the performance of other KPIs across fat-content categories.

**Visualization:** Donut Chart

The analysis compares sales generated by low-fat and regular-fat products.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales

**Objective:**  
Compare total sales across different outlets segmented by fat content.

**Visualization:** Stacked Column / Bar Chart

### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment

**Objective:**  
Evaluate how the age or establishment year of an outlet influences total sales.

**Visualization:** Line Chart

The dashboard tracks sales across outlet establishment years to identify changes and patterns in sales performance over time.

## Key Insights

The dashboard provides several business-oriented observations:

- **Total sales reached approximately $1.2M**, providing a high-level view of overall sales performance.
- **Average sales were approximately $141**, indicating the average revenue generated per sale.
- **8,523 items** were included in the analysis.
- The overall **average customer rating was 4.0**, indicating the general level of customer satisfaction represented in the dataset.
- **Fruits and Vegetables and Snack Foods** appear among the strongest-performing item categories by total sales.
- Sales vary considerably across **outlet locations and outlet sizes**, highlighting the importance of location and outlet characteristics in sales performance.
- **Tier 3 outlets** show the highest total sales contribution among the three location tiers in the dashboard.
- Outlet establishment year shows fluctuations in sales performance, with the highest sales visible around **2018**.
- The comparison of outlet types helps identify differences in sales, item volume, and average sales across store formats.
